## 1. What the extension does

ShikimoriSync is a Seanime extension that synchronizes your anime/manga list between AniList and Shikimori. It reads your AniList list, reads your Shikimori list, and sends the differences you choose (scores, statuses, progress, notes) to Shikimori via Shikimori's official API.
